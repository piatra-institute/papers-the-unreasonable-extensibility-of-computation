# Editorial record

## Reverse outline

1. *Introduction* — the console runtime and its documented requirements, and the sense in which the console could always do this.
2. *Senses of machine capability* — physical possibility, computability in principle, resource-bounded feasibility and operational capability; the feasible set and the three frontiers.
3. *Scope of the title* — the relation to Wigner and to Halevy, Norvig and Pereira, and the three aspects that need explanation.
4. *Requirements of the console runtime* — memory arithmetic, the file-size check, the predicted streaming, the technique lattice, the earlier revision, and the shift from storage-bound to arithmetic-bound.
5. *Continuous improvement and discrete capability* — the algorithm census, other efficiency series, the calibrated mixture model, and amplification by distance from budget.
6. *Known and deployable capability* — stack depth, stationary and simulated deployable shares, waning interest, and required component lifetimes.
7. *Limitations* — what the results do not imply.
8. *Falsification tests* — how each result could be checked or refuted.
